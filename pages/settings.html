      Strategy for : the optimiser's ways to ride it. Choose before the start; a race already under way keeps its plan until it ends.

      Look
      Pacing
      Field

      Gaps are measured where both riders have been, every 50 m of the event, so they compare like with like. The trend (/min) is the time you gain or lose on each rider a minute, over the last four minutes, so a sprint barely moves it (over the last minute until four have passed). At that pace, carried to the line from your plan's time to go, each rider's tag says how it ends: you beat / beats you by so much, or outlined, close when you finish: in reach ahead, pressure behind (on the road gap: catch / catches you and when). Riders who finish nowhere near you fade back. The line under the header sums it up: Clear (ride your plan), Defend, Chase or Contested, with your place heading for (4th → 3rd). Click gap for the gaps and places predicted at the finish.

      Rivals

      Behind you on the left, ahead on the right: the nearest rider each side, then the nearest that matters. Each says how it ends at the pace of the last four minutes (a sprint barely moves it): catch 4:10 (you catch them in 4:10) or catches you 3:20; outlined, close when you finish: in reach 8 s (a little more from you, or a fade from them, and you catch them) or pressure 5 s (they finish 5 s behind you). Beside it, how much you gain (green) or lose (orange) on them a minute. In the middle, your place and the verdict on the finish (as the field's): clear, defend, chase or contested, and the place you are heading for (→ 3rd).

      On the track, riders you meet before the finish are ringed and those close at the finish ring-dashed; the track widens to hold both. The ends count who you catch and who catches you, and the riders beyond the range (+3). Close is a second for each minute to go, 5 s to 30 s.
