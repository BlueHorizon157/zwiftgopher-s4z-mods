# 0.9.3: mass-start races, a plan out of step with its race, one plan per course

Works with any planner. A plan made since the planner's lap-end update (2026-11-12) starts at the
timing line on the routes this release is about; plan the race again if yours is older.

## Fixed

- **Targets that changed late, all race.** On routes whose lap does not join up in the planner's
  route data (the Watopia Hilly Route and Flat Route among some fifty), a plan started a few hundred
  metres before the race did. The mod finds the timing line by matching your position to the
  plan's course, but it would not believe a line more than 300 m in, so on such a plan it fell
  back to distance alone and every target changed late: 425 m late on a Hilly Route race, half a
  minute to two minutes, with the countdowns and the time gained or lost per interval off to
  match. The road is now believed up to 1.5 km in, so the targets change where the plan means them
  to, and the HUD says "plan off by 429 m" so you know to make the plan again.
- **Mass-start races: the finish time is the result's.** In a mass start the game starts its
  distance and timer at a timing line a little way out of the pen, as in a TT, and the plan starts
  there. The result counts from the gun. The mod now runs the plan from the line and adds the
  seconds from the gun to the line to the finish time, the predicted finish and the field's
  clocks, so they read as the results will (11:44 on the Hilly Route race against ZwiftPower's
  11:44.9). Ahead or behind the plan is the same on either clock. The banner said "clock from the
  gun" while counting from the line; it now says "gun to line 0:18".
- **The field in a mass start** ran each rider from the line while calling it the gun; it now
  runs everyone from the gun, when Sauce gives the event's start time.

- **One plan per course in the plan list.** Several versions of the same plan piled up in
  settings → Plans when you sent a plan again from the planner, or made one with Plan it. The list
  now keeps one plan for each course (a route with its laps, or with its distance): a newer plan
  for the course replaces the older one, and the active plan stays active through the swap. A
  plan the planner no longer has in Sauce (replaced there, or removed from its new Plans in Sauce
  list) leaves the list on the next sync. Plans from here are kept apart. The list now shows each
  plan's laps or distance next to the route.

## Changed

- **Two new notices in the banner while racing.** "plan off by 429 m": the plan's timing line is
  not where the road has it (hover for which end of the race is not in the plan). "course not
  matched: targets by distance": the plan has a course and the road has not been matched to it
  half a kilometre in.
- Before a mass start the banner says "ready, mass start"; hover for what that means for the
  clock.

## Notes

- Checked against a recorded mass-start race on the Watopia Hilly Route (6.1 km, no draft), with
  the plan as it was raced and with the same plan from the updated planner: every target within
  1.5 s of the road on both, and 704.9 s from the gun against the result's 704.888 s.
- New plans mark the stretches the planner draws at the end of a lap that does not join up; the
  mod takes no position fix there and keeps its place through them.
