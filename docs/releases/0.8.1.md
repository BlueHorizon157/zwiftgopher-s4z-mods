# 0.8.1: strategies any time, plans where they belong, Plan from here again

Fixes and changes from the first rides on 0.8.0. Works with the same planner as 0.8.0.

## Changed

- **A plan comes up where it belongs.** A plan arriving from the planner loads by itself only
  in its event's pen, or on its route on a free ride. Anywhere else the HUD offers it
  (*new plan: … · 14:57*, **Use**), also with no plan loaded, and **Use** loads it now. On a
  free ride with nothing loaded, the newest plan for the route you are on comes up. Joining a
  pen with no plan for it puts away a plan made for another course rather than racing it
  there (load it again from the list to ride it anyway).
- **Change the strategy during a race.** *Fastest*, *Balanced* or *Safe finish*, any time: the
  top line's strategy chip (or **Plan** in the tray) opens the plan panel. During a race the
  targets change in place; the clock, your position and your W′ carry on, and the interval you
  are in starts its statistics again.
- **Plan from here starts on Balanced**, keeping some W′ back while the tiredness estimate is
  new. Settings → Advanced → *Plan from here starts on* picks Fastest or Safe finish instead.

## New

- **Clear plan** in the plan panel: ride with no plan, then *Plan from here* (or *Plan it* in a
  pen). The pen or the route does not bring the cleared plan back.
- **Plan again from here**: with a plan from here running, the plan panel offers it again from
  where you are now. The planner replays the whole ride, so the new plan starts from the W′
  you have left.

## Fixed

- A plan edited in the planner and sent again under the same code now replaces the one in the
  HUD before the start: the targets and the finish time are the new plan's. 0.8.0 kept the old
  one until the strategy changed or the window reopened. An edit that changed only the times
  (weight, CdA, bike) also counts as a new plan now.
