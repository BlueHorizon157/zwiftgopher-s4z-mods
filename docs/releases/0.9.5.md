# 0.9.5: the field's finish line, and who is still in play

Works with the same planner as 0.9.4.

## Fixed

- **The field and the rivals strip finished everyone early.** If you had the mod open on a warm-up
  route before you joined the pen, the field could keep the warm-up route's length as the race's.
  The rider list and your own data reach the mod a second apart, and the first list in the race
  arrived with the old length. On a CTT round on Scotland Smash (18.07 km) every rider showed
  "finished" from 15.37 km on: you "finished 18th of 20 so far" with 2.2 km still to ride, and the
  finish times and places were wrong to match. The finish line now comes only with your race's
  own length, and follows it. The HUD and the stack were not affected.

## Changed

- **Who you can still catch, and who can still catch you, counts the riders' own distance to go.**
  The gap to a rider is measured at the point you have both reached. When they are behind you on
  the road, it goes on moving after you finish: they still have the road from there to the line
  to ride against your time on it. The finish outlook carried every gap over your own time to go
  only. Now a rider 20 s up with a minute to go, while you gain 5 s a minute, is out of reach
  when they are ahead of you on the road (or level), and caught when they still have 5 minutes to
  ride. A rider behind you on the clock who is gaining on you is a threat for their whole
  stretch, not just your last minute. "N s a minute faster catches them" is still on your own
  minutes: the rest of their stretch is road you have already ridden. After your finish, riders
  still out on the course are carried to the line too, instead of standing still.
