# 0.9.6: Sauce's other windows stop flickering

Works with the same planner as 0.9.5.

## Fixed

- **Sauce's Watching window reloaded, and Nearby and Groups flickered, while the mod was open.**
  The mod kept the state its windows share (the plan library, the active plan, the bias and
  offset, the planner link) in Sauce's global settings, and Sauce hands every change there to
  every one of its windows as a change to that window's settings: the Watching window reloads its
  whole page, and Nearby and Groups redraw everything. With the planner linked, the mod's check for
  new plans wrote there about once a minute, and the recorder every couple of seconds while it was
  on. The mod now keeps its shared state in the browser storage Sauce's windows share but don't
  watch. Your plans, the active plan, the bias and offset and the planner link come across the
  first time each window starts; there is nothing to do.
