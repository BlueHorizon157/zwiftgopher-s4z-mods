# 0.9.9: a full check of the mod, and what it found

Works with the same planner as 0.9.8. Every part of the mod was gone through for this release:
the race engine, the plans and the link, the field and rivals windows, and the HUD, list and
settings. These are the fixes. Nothing you set up needs doing again.

## In the race

- **Joining a race already under way works.** The mod waited for you to be seen at the start
  line, and a rider who joined late, or whose connection dropped them out of the event and
  back, never was: the HUD sat waiting for the whole race. It now follows the race 30 seconds
  after you join (sooner once Zwift has you on the course), and coming back to the same
  category within 20 seconds is the same race, not a new one.
- **A gap in the data over the end of an interval no longer makes you look slow.** When the
  ticks from Zwift stalled for a few seconds just as you crossed into the next interval, the
  whole gap went to the interval you had left, and your ahead/behind was out for minutes. On
  Crit Cross a 6-second stall put it 24 seconds out. The crossing is now timed where it
  happened.
- **One stray reading of "0 m to go" no longer ends the race.** The finish by Sauce's distance
  to go now also needs you to be at the event's length.
- **Reset, or a new plan, after the finish no longer races the same event again.** Zwift's
  timer keeps running after the line, and a Reset there started a second race on it that
  finished 30 seconds later. The next race now starts at a real start.
- **Leaving the pen for the plan's route** arms the plan on the route, as on any free ride. It
  stayed waiting for the event.
- **The finish shows the finish's own time and ahead/behind**, not the last reading before it:
  on Crit Cross, 9.8 s behind where it showed 10.7.
- **A power dropout no longer drags an interval's average down.** Seconds with no power reading
  counted as 0 W; the average is now over the seconds that had one.
- **The same plan sent again with a new course, finish, CP or W′** (after a planner update, or
  new numbers of yours) is taken up. The mod saw the same intervals and kept the old one.
- **On a free ride, the length of the route's lap is no longer taken for the race's.** The HUD
  could say the plan was the wrong distance and put the timing line and finish in the wrong
  place.
- **A mass start's start time is kept** when Sauce gave it before the mod saw your category.

## Your plans and the link

- **Linking again, or unlinking, keeps your plans.** A new link has a new shelf in the planner,
  and the plans the old one had brought were deleted at the first sync. The settings page has
  always said "the plans here stay"; now they do.
- **Nothing changes under you in a race.** A sync with the planner that began in the pen could
  finish after the start and remove or replace the plan you were riding. A sync now changes
  nothing once the race is on (it is done after the race), the plan you have loaded is never
  removed because the planner stopped listing it, and an expired plan stays usable, as the
  settings page says.
- **A plan whose download failed arrives at the next try.** It never came until the planner
  changed something else.
- **The plan you have loaded is never the one dropped** when the library is full (ten plans).
  Plans from here go first, then the oldest.
- **A plan you remove by hand stays removed.** It came back at the next sync while the planner
  still listed it. Publish it again in the planner and it comes back.
- **A plan you picked by hand in the pen stays** when you open another window there.
- **The line about the next TT updates as soon as a plan arrives.** It said *no plan yet* for up
  to a minute.
- **Replaying a recording no longer changes your real plans.** A replay window works on a copy.
- **A planner that could not be reached is tried again after ten minutes**, not only after
  Sauce restarts. An answer that stops arriving says *the planner did not answer in time*, not
  *HTTP 200*.
- **The event's kind and start are asked again** when Sauce did not have them yet, and again
  after you rejoin.
- **Kept race recordings**: leaving the pen and joining again no longer ends the recording early,
  and a recording that failed to save is tried again.
- **The update check** asks GitHub once a day for all the windows together (each window asked
  when Sauce started them together), and gives up after 8 seconds if GitHub does not answer.

## The field and the rivals strip

- **While you watch another rider in Zwift you stay in the middle**, and the finish outlook pauses
  (*spectating*). The rider you were watching took your place.
- **The field finishes where the HUD does.** On a course whose finish has been measured, riders
  finish there, not at Sauce's route length (84 m too long on Crit Cross), so their times and
  your gaps to the finishers are right. When Sauce has no length for the route at all (a route
  newer than your Sauce), the plan's finish is used and riders finish; before, nobody did.
- **A rider who leaves the event before the finish** is no longer compared with you for as long
  as they ride on: they sit below the line, *left the event*.
- **A rider who crosses the line out of sight** and is seen again just past it now has a finish
  time.
- **In a mass start every rider's clock runs from the gun.** Riders first seen before the
  event's start time was known kept a clock from the line.
- **"At the finish" is blank until there is a time to go**, instead of showing the gap now under
  that heading.
- **Outside an event the field and strip show nothing**, as the README said. With *every
  category* on, the field shows the categories of your event, not every rider on the map or the
  finishers of your last event.
- **The verdict names only riders with a pace**, as the strip's colours do; and *N s a minute
  faster catches them* is no longer worked out in the last 30 seconds.
- **After you finish: *2nd of 3 seen so far***: the count is the riders the mod saw cross the
  line, and says so.
- A Reset clears the field's and the strip's order and sticky states from the last race.

## The HUD, the list and the settings

- **The HUD's code box takes a pasted planner link**, as the HUD says it does. It kept the first
  six characters (*https:*).
- **The settings page when the planner cannot be reached** says so (*Can't reach the planner
  right now*) and tries again every 30 seconds. It used to say the planner did not offer
  linking, and Unlink, Sync now, Cancel and Remove did nothing until the page was opened again.
  Unlink now works offline too.
- **A strategy picked after the finish is for the next ride**, as the panel says: the buttons
  work and the choice is ridden at the next start. They were greyed out.
- **The dial's *new plan* offer fits inside the circle.** Its first letters and its × were cut
  off. The × reads *Not now* when no plan is loaded.
- **The strip at 130 px or taller shows cadence, heart rate and speed side by side.** Only the
  cadence showed.
- **Nothing in the dial or stack is cut off mid-word**: the banner says *ready, waiting*,
  *wrong plan length*, *from here* where there is no room, with the full words in the tooltip,
  and the stack's strategy chip comes first so it is always there. In the dial the chip sits
  at the top while racing.
- **The strip's first screen fits** with a long event name for the next TT.
- **A plan with only one strategy** no longer says *Pick a strategy*.
- **Esc closes the plan panel** however it was opened.
- **A plan loaded away from its course** says *not on its route yet*, not *waiting for Zwift*.
- **The keyboard reaches the tray** (offset, bias, Reset, Plan), and the settings page keeps your
  place while it updates.
- Plan times are rounded to the second as the planner rounds them, so the HUD, the list and the
  strategy buttons show the same time (*14:55*, not *14:54* on one and *14:55* on another).
  *Next in* reads *1:40* past 99 seconds, not *1680 s*. The list's header says what its time is
  (*plan 14:55*, *finished 14:47*). No more *−0 W* or *-0.0%*.
- Spectating in the dial shows the rider's name, with the rings dimmed. The update notice shows
  in the dial after the finish.
- The bias never goes to 0 %.
