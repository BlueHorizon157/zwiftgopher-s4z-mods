# 0.9.2: rivals coloured by who is in play

Works with the same planner as 0.9.0.

In short: **green = chase, red = defend**. Green riders are always ahead of you and red always
behind; the side says where they are, the colour whether they can change your place at the
finish, and the seconds a minute which way the gap is moving.

## Changed

- **The rivals strip colours only the riders in play.** Green is a target: a rider ahead of you on
  the clock who, at the pace of the last four minutes, you finish ahead of or close behind. Red is
  a threat: a rider behind you who, at that pace, finishes ahead of you or close behind. Close is a
  second per minute to go, 5 to 30 s. Everyone else stays plain, so a rider far behind and falling
  away, or far ahead and pulling away, no longer shows green or red. The colour is on the rider's
  name and their dot on the track; the seconds a minute beside the name keep their sign and no
  colour.
- **A call waits for a pace.** A rider is called once there is a pace for them (90 s of shared
  history), and the colours are faint until the finish outlook settles (20 minutes of racing, or
  five minutes to go). Once you have finished, nobody is called. Hover a name or a dot for the
  call in words: "at this pace you finish 4.2 s ahead of them: a target".
