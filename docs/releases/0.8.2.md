# 0.8.2 (unreleased): a countdown that counts in seconds

Works with the same planner as 0.8.1. Plans made after the planner's update of
10 November 2026 carry where each interval's time goes, and the countdown uses it.

## Fixed

- **Time to the next change counts down in real time.** It was the distance left over your
  speed at that moment, so on a hill inside an interval it leapt up (half the speed, twice the
  seconds) and on the crest it collapsed. Now it is the plan's time for the rest of the
  interval, adjusted for the power you have ridden in it (a few seconds' dip hardly moves it,
  riding under the target the whole way does) and for how your speed has compared with the
  plan's over the last five minutes (a draft is learnt). It eases onto that estimate a second
  a second and still reaches zero at the change. The dial's time ring, the HUD's progress bar
  and the list's interval bar follow the same clock. On four recorded TTs the figure went up
  or jumped three seconds or more on about a fifth of the ticks before, under 1 % now, and its
  error against when the interval really ended halved (12.7 s to 6.1 s on average).
- **The countdown knows where an interval's time goes.** The planner now writes a time mark
  every 200 m inside each interval into the plan (and into each strategy card), and the
  countdown counts the rest of the interval by them: a flat interval that ends on a climb no
  longer runs short near the end. On the same four TTs its error went from 6.1 s to 3.4 s
  (Innsbruck, with long mixed intervals, 15.9 s to 6.8 s). The ahead/behind figure uses the
  same plan time, so inside a long interval with a climb in it it swings about half as much
  (Innsbruck: at worst 35 s, was 69 s). Plans made
  before have no marks and count as before.
