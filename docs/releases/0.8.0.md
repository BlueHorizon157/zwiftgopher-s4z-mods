# 0.8.0: link to the planner, plans arrive by themselves, Plan it

Needs the planner deploy of November 2026 (TT/RIDE_DAY_PLAN.md in the main repo); against an
older planner the mod loads codes exactly as 0.7 did and says linking is not offered yet.

## New

- **Link to the planner** (settings → Plans): a six-letter pairing code, typed once into the
  planner's Share dialog. From then on every plan you optimise there arrives in the mod within
  a minute while a HUD window is open and no race is running. With no plan loaded it becomes
  the plan; with one loaded the HUD offers it (**Use**) before the start. The planner shows
  *Sauce has it ✓*. The link is a token the mod keeps; the planner keeps only its hash; either
  side can end it. No API keys.
- **The plan for the event.** A plan made from the planner's event picker knows its event: when
  you join that pen the HUD arms it by itself before the start, else the plan for the same
  route with the same laps or within 1 % of a distance event's length. A plan you choose by
  hand after joining stays.
- **Next TT** in the plan panel: the next time trial you are signed up for (what Sauce has
  loaded, about four hours ahead, and the events the planner sent plans for), with whether a
  plan fits it; the settings page lists them.
- **Plan it**: linked, with a signed-up event or in the pen without a plan, one button asks the
  planner for an optimised plan with your last planner settings (or its defaults: settings →
  Advanced → *Plan it uses*); the HUD arms it in a few seconds. Six an hour, twenty a day.
- The mod tells the planner which events you signed up for, so its picker marks them.

## Changed

- **The dial, reworked.** A 270° gauge open at the bottom, so the next change sits in the
  opening clear of every ring, and each line of text has its own row inside the inner ring.
  The middle ring is now the time left in the interval, draining to the change and amber in
  the warning time (it was a thin progress ring that filled up). The target is a bold tick
  on the power ring and the next target a dashed one; the W′ floor is an amber tick. A
  darker disc behind the numbers (smoke and dark backgrounds), larger and heavier type, and
  before the start the strategy chip sits by the plan time (*fastest 14:54*).
- **Time to go.** While racing the HUD and the interval list show the predicted time left to
  the finish line (*to go 10:24*) with the delta from the plan beside it (*on plan*, *ahead
  0:12*, *behind 0:40*; on the dial on its own line). Settings → Pacing → *Finish shows*
  switches back to the predicted finish time.
- **The rivals track** reaches ±1 min by default (it fitted the whole field, which crowded
  the riders near you) and widens for the riders in play: one you catch, or who catches you,
  before the finish at the current trend, ringed on the track. Riders beyond the range are
  counted at its ends. Settings → Rivals → *Track range*: ±30 s, ±1, ±2, ±5 min or the whole
  field.
- **How it ends with each rider.** The field and the rivals strip tag the riders that matter
  at the pace of the last four minutes (a least-squares fit, so a sprint barely moves it; the
  one-minute trend could bring half the field into play): *catch 4:10* / *catches you 3:20*
  when you meet before the finish (on the clock in the field: *you beat* / *beats you*), and
  outlined, riders close when you finish: *in reach 8 s* ahead, *pressure 5 s* behind (a
  second per minute to go, 5–30 s). Tags hold on for a margin so a rider on the edge does
  not flicker. The strip's second rider each side is now the nearest that matters, and the
  track ends count who you catch and who catches you. The trend figures (/min, *gaining 1.2
  s/min*) use the same four-minute pace.
- **Install fix.** Two shipped files were both called `field.mjs` (the field window's and the
  field model's). Copying files rather than the whole folder on Windows asks to replace one
  with the other, which broke the field window. The model is now `engine/field-model.mjs`,
  and a test keeps every shipped file name unique.
- The library keeps ten plans (was five).
- `page-check.mjs --probe` (the Sauce probe) and `--links` (the link states).
