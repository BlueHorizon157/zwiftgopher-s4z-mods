# 0.9.7: the race starts at the line after a warm-up

Works with the same planner as 0.9.6.

## Fixed

- **A plan loaded on a warm-up raced from the moment you joined the event, and the pen didn't
  reset it.** Sauce reports the event a few seconds before Zwift moves you to the pen, and
  for those seconds you are still on the warm-up road with its distance and timer. The mod took
  that for a race already 12.8 km along and started the clock. The clock then ran while you
  waited in the pen, so the prediction drifted to *behind*, and you had to press Reset after the
  start. Now nothing is placed or started between joining and the pen. The HUD waits in the pen
  (*ready, waiting for the start*) and the clock starts at the timing line.
- **Back in the pen while racing, the race is armed again.** If the clock is running and you are
  at the start of the event (Zwift's timer and distance at zero) for two seconds, the race so far
  is cleared and the clock starts again at the line. One stray tick at zero doesn't count.
- **Reset in the middle of a race now takes Zwift's race timer.** The clock used to restart from
  the metres past the line, never more than 10 s back, so a reset well after the line started
  the clock too late. A plan loaded mid-race does the same.
- **The ahead/behind prediction no longer panics in the first minutes.** It timed the rest of the
  race from your power against the targets alone, and trusted a few seconds of it: cross the line
  already at speed, ride the first interval under its target and still on time, and it said 36 s
  behind for a race you finished 4 s ahead. It now also uses how fast you are going against the
  plan for the power you ride (as the interval countdown does), leans on the plan for longer, and
  leaves out your first 30 s after the line, so it says nothing about the finish until then. On
  four recorded races it is about twice as close in the first five minutes, and it now picks up a
  plan that is a few percent too fast or too slow for you (a draft, the wrong bike) within minutes.

## Rivals

- The track under the names now reaches **±30 s** either side of you by default, so riders in a
  close fight spread out instead of piling up on your marker. A rivals window you have opened
  before keeps the range it saved (±1 min unless you changed it): change it in Settings → Rivals →
  *Track range*. ±1, ±2 and ±5 min and the whole field are still there.
- The coloured dots (green a target, red a threat) sit on top of the others and your own, so a
  rider in play is never hidden in a crowd.
- The rate beside each rider is coloured by its sign, as in the field window: **+ s a minute**
  green (you gain on them), **− s a minute** red (you lose).
