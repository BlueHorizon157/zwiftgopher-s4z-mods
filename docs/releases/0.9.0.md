# 0.9.0: where you are on the course, and a field you can compare

The intervals now switch where the plan puts them on the road. That needs a plan that carries its
course, so plan the race again after the planner's course update; plans made before it still load
and ride on the old rule, as in 0.8.2.

## Before you ride

- **Plan the race again.** A plan made after the planner's course update carries the course
  (zg-tt-plan/1, `route.course`), and the mod matches where you are on it. On the routes measured
  from real rides (California Dreamin', Innsbruck short lap, Everything Bagel, Tempus Fugit,
  Legends and Lava) the plan also starts at the game's timing line and ends at the finish, so its
  times read like the game's.
- **A plan without a course** keeps the old rule for the intervals (see the notes at the end).

## Fixed

- **Intervals switched late on a TT with a timing line inside the route data.** On California
  Dreamin' the mod took the start of the route data as the line, so every interval switched 10 to
  35 s late (median about 15 s), most on the climb. The line is now found from where you ride,
  on the planner's own course, and the intervals switch where you reach them: on that ride within
  1.2 s of the road for all seventeen changes, and the clock at the finish read 2095.6 s against
  the game's 2095 s.
- **A finish past the plan's end.** The race now finishes at the event's length: the clock stops
  at the line you finish at, the last interval closes there with its W′, and the plan's pace
  carries on to the finish.
- **Leaving the event after the finish no longer clears the result.** Your time and the field's
  places stay put when the game drops you from the event, and a new event clears them.
- **The race no longer ends at the start line when Sauce does not know the route.** Sauce ships
  its routes with each release; on a route newer than your Sauce it has no length for the event,
  and 0.8.2 and earlier took that as nothing left to ride, so the HUD said the race was over as you
  crossed the line. The race now runs on to the finish.
- **A rider who is first seen after the line** no longer gets a time from a rough guess: they show
  as finished with no time beyond 100 m past the line.

## Changed

- **The field compares like for like.** Every gap is measured at the same distance along the course
  on the clock, and nothing is ranked by the road any more: the road gap, the catch and pressure
  tags and the road-based play range are gone. A place is a rank among the riders you can compare
  with, you included. Riders not yet comparable, or not seen for two minutes, sit below a divider
  with the reason.
- **Finished riders keep their time.** A rider's time is frozen at the line on their own clock. The
  place column is one numbering for every row (the place on the clock); a finisher's place among the
  finishers is in its finish cell ("finished 2nd") and a finisher is never dropped from the list.
- **Plain words.** The rivals strip and the verdict say "seconds a minute", not "/min", and a
  finisher whose time was not seen says so instead of showing 0:00.
- **The finish outlook waits.** No predicted place and no verdict until 20 minutes of racing or five
  minutes to go: before that the middle of the rivals strip shows your place only, so the early
  calls that were wrong no longer show.
- **The rivals track** reads ±30 s, ±1 min (the default), ±2 min, ±5 min or the whole field, and its
  ends count the riders beyond the range on each side.
- **The dial's current target is a bold green tick**, so it reads apart from the next target's
  dashed tick.
- **Settings move on.** A field set to the road gap moves to the clock, and a rivals track set to
  the old play range moves to ±1 min. The settings page is written in plainer words.

## Notes

- The course is used only in an event. Free rides and plans from here keep the old rule, and a
  race with no position data does too.
- **Changes for every TT ride, not only the ones with a course.** On a plan without a course the
  clock's start is now placed at the timing line (not at the rider's metre), so the predicted
  finish and time shift for the start; and when the event has a length, the finish comes from
  Sauce's distance to go, so a plan whose end is past the event's finish ends at the finish. The
  plan-metre rule for intervals is unchanged on those plans.
- **A stretch without position** (the feed drops the rider's lat/lng for up to 600 m of odometer
  after a lock) keeps the metre on the odometer from the last match. Only before the first lock does
  a race run on the old rule with no course.
- On the measured ride the course matched the road to within 6 m on every race tick after it
  locked, 38 m into the event.
