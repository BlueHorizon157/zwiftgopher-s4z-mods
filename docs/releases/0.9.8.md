# 0.9.8: races kept for reporting a problem

Works with the same planner as 0.9.7.

## New

- **Keep race recordings** (Settings → Advanced). Turn it on once and every event you ride is
  kept for 14 days: from ten minutes before you join it to two minutes after the finish, with
  what the mod showed and said along the way. Nothing to remember before or after a race. When
  the mod gets something wrong, open Settings, find the race in the list and press *Save*: you
  get a `.jsonl.gz` file (a few MB) to send with your report, which replays the race exactly as
  you rode it. Each race is saved two minutes after the finish (or after you leave the event);
  if Sauce was closed during a race, it is saved the next time Sauce runs. Off by default.
- *Record telemetry* still records everything for as long as it is on, as before.

## Note

- Updating clears what *Record telemetry* had kept so far. If you have a recording you want,
  save it before you update.
