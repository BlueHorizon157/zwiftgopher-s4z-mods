# 0.9.8: races kept for reporting a problem; the real finish on measured courses

Works with the same planner as 0.9.7. The finish below needs plans made on the planner from
2026-11-23 on.

## New

- **Keep race recordings** (Settings → Advanced). Turn it on once and every event you ride is
  kept for 14 days: from ten minutes before you join it to two minutes after the finish, with
  what the mod showed and said along the way. Nothing to remember before or after a race. When
  the mod gets something wrong, open Settings, find the race in the list and press *Save*: you
  get a `.jsonl.gz` file (a few MB) to send with your report, which replays the race exactly as
  you rode it. Each race is saved two minutes after the finish (or after you leave the event);
  if Sauce was closed during a race, it is saved the next time Sauce runs. Off by default.
  Sauce keeps each window profile's settings apart: turn it on in every profile you race in.
- *Record telemetry* still records everything for as long as it is on, as before.

## Fixed

- **The ahead/behind no longer jumps a long way ahead in the first minutes when the plan's
  timing line was out.** When the plan has the line in the wrong place, the mod finds the right
  one on the road a few seconds after the start. Until now the stretch it jumped over counted
  as distance you had ridden. On Crit Cross, where the plan had the line 69 m early, it said
  33 s ahead after a minute of a race you finished 9 s behind. It is now about 8 s out at that
  point, and over five recorded races its first five minutes are 9 s out on average, from 13.
  Races where the plan had the line right are unchanged.
- **The race ends at the real finish on courses whose finish has been measured.** The mod used
  to end the race where Sauce's distance to go ran out, and Sauce's idea of a route's length can
  be wrong: 84 m too long on Crit Cross, so the mod showed 24:15 for a race ZwiftPower timed at
  24:08. On a plan whose finish the planner has measured from races (plans made from
  2026-11-23 on), the race now ends at that finish, timed to the tenth. Other plans end as
  before.

## Note

- Updating clears what *Record telemetry* had kept so far. If you have a recording you want,
  save it before you update.
