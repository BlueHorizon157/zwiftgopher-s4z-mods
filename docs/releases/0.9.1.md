# 0.9.1: a race that starts, and a field that fits

Works with the same planner as 0.9.0. A plan made since the planner's course update carries the
course; plan the race again if yours is older.

## Fixed

- **The race ended at the start line** when Sauce did not know the event's route. Sauce ships its
  list of routes with each release, so on a route newer than your Sauce (California Dreamin', for
  one) it has no length for the event. 0.8.2 and earlier took that as nothing left to ride, and the
  HUD said the race was over as you crossed the line. 0.9.0 already rides on to the finish in that
  case; 0.9.1 adds a test that replays a real race without the event's length, so it stays that
  way. Updating Sauce as well gives the mod the event's length for the finish.
- **The field fits in Sauce's font.** "finished 8th" no longer loses its last letters (in a narrow
  window it runs into the empty trend column), a rate of 10 s a minute or more reads in whole
  seconds ("+14 s"), and a rider not compared yet shows their name and the reason in full
  ("not started").
- **The stack HUD's plan panel fits its window.** The plan-code row wraps, so Close and Clear plan
  no longer sit outside the narrow window.

## Notes

- Checked in Sauce 2.4.0-alpha.5 by replaying a recorded California Dreamin' TT through every
  window: the clock read 2095.6 s at the finish against the game's 2095 s, all 18 intervals
  closed, no console errors.
